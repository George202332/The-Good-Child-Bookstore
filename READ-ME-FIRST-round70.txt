ROUND 70: CHECKOUT SHIPPING STEP NEVER STALLS SILENTLY
No database change this round (no migration). No files to delete.

What changed
- Checkout delivery step: "Getting your shipping price..." with a spinner; Continue shows its own spinner and is never dead.
- After about 5 seconds: "This is taking longer than usual, please wait."
- One automatic retry, then a clear message with a Try again button (typed details are kept).
- If a field blocks Next, the reason appears beside that exact field.
- Server logs every slow or failed price request (kind, country, time, book count; no personal data).
- Site Health shows fine/slow/failed counts under Lulu.
- Admin Books table and Lulu Publishing tab flag books with incomplete print details.
- Staging-only test switch LULU_QUOTE_SIMULATE (slow / fail / postcode). Ignored on the live site. Leave unset normally.
